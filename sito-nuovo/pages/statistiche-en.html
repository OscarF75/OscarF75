How to read these numbers

A view is a site page load. If the same person reloads the page, the count may increase. We do not measure unique people, visit duration or identity. Opening a slide on the page does not add a view.

Aggregated data only. Age and gender are unavailable: the site neither knows nor infers them. Statistics do not store IP addresses, precise coordinates, visitor identifiers, full browser strings or individual histories. We use no cookies or identifiers for this count.

Indicative counts. We exclude recognisable bots, recognised previews and requests with DNT/GPC signals. Viewing this dashboard does not increase counts. Your own visits to the site may be counted when you browse without signing in. Some bots may evade filtering. Initial counts may include technical publication checks. An iPad in desktop mode may appear as a computer; VPNs and mobile networks can affect the reported area.

Incomplete source information. WhatsApp, some apps and browsers may not report the referring site: these visits appear as “Direct / unavailable”. No personal searches or keywords are recorded.

Available history: 400 days. Older aggregates are deleted on the next visit or report view. Clicks are aggregated by day and action type, with no individual histories. Data refreshes when you press “Refresh” and every minute while this page is open. Statistics are available on any device through this link, without an account or password. Anyone who receives the link can view them.

Collection has been active since this feature was published. No earlier history has been recovered.
